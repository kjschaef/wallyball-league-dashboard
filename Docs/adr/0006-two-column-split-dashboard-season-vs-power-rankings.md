# Two-Column Split Dashboard: Season Race vs. League Power Rankings

To cleanly resolve the conceptual tension between quarterly tournament standings (reset each quarter, evaluated on Win% and point differential) and permanent skill ratings (career rolling Elo), the primary dashboard adopts a two-column desktop split and a 1-tap top segmented switcher on mobile. The left zone is dedicated to the active Season Race (selector, performance trend chart, and season standings), while the right zone displays the League Power Rankings (Top 3 podium medals, Elo ladder, skill tiers, and weekly movers).
